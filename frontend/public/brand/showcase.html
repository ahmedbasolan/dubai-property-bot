The Engine

7-Factor Investment Scoring

Every community is scored on 7 metrics that explain 85% of investment returns. Each factor is weighted by its historical correlation to ROI.

Gross Yield

WEIGHT: 25%

Annual rental income relative to property price. Higher gross yield means faster capital recovery.

Net Yield

WEIGHT: 25%

After service charges, management fees, and vacancy loss. The true return on your investment.

Price / sqft

WEIGHT: 20%

Below average price per square foot signals undervalued opportunity in the community.

Service Charges

WEIGHT: 15%

Lower annual service charges mean more money stays in your pocket as net return.

Supply Pipeline

WEIGHT: 15%

New units incoming create supply risk. Communities with heavy pipeline face downward pressure.

Occupancy Rate

WEIGHT: 10%

High occupancy signals strong rental demand. Low occupancy is an early warning sign.

Developer Track Record

WEIGHT: 10%

Proven developers deliver on time and quality. New builders carry execution risk.
