# Dubai Marina Market Report - Q1 2024

## Executive Summary

Dubai Marina remains one of the most sought-after waterfront communities, attracting young professionals and investors alike. The area saw a 20% increase in transaction volume in Q1 2024, with prices stabilizing after the 2022-2023 surge.

## Key Metrics

- **Average Price per sqft:** AED 1,600 - AED 2,200
- **Average 1BR Price:** AED 1.2M - AED 1.5M
- **Average 2BR Price:** AED 1.8M - AED 2.5M
- **Rental Yield:** 7.0% - 8.0%
- **Occupancy Rate:** 89%
- **Transaction Volume:** Up 20% vs Q4 2023

## Price Trends

Prices in Dubai Marina have moderated after the sharp increases of 2022-2023. The community offers better value per sqft compared to Downtown Dubai while maintaining strong rental demand. Studios and 1BR units are particularly popular among investors targeting young professional tenants.

## Investment Highlights

1. **Waterfront Living:** Premium lifestyle at competitive prices
2. **Strong Rental Market:** High demand from expat professionals
3. **Excellent Connectivity:** Near Sheikh Zayed Road and Dubai Metro
4. **Walk-to-Beach Access:** JBR Beach within walking distance

## Risk Factors

- Aging buildings may require higher maintenance costs
- Traffic congestion during peak hours
- Competition from newer communities like Dubai Harbour

## Recommendation

Dubai Marina offers excellent value for investors seeking waterfront properties with strong yields. Studios under AED 1M and 1BR units represent the sweet spot for rental income. Consider newer buildings (post-2018) for lower service charges and better amenities.
