# Dubai Hills Estate Market Report - Q1 2024

## Executive Summary

Dubai Hills Estate, developed by Emaar, has become the go-to master-planned community for families seeking quality living at competitive prices. The community saw strong off-plan activity in Q1 2024, with several new phases launched.

## Key Metrics

- **Average Price per sqft:** AED 1,400 - AED 1,900
- **Average 1BR Price:** AED 1.1M - AED 1.4M
- **Average 2BR Price:** AED 1.7M - AED 2.2M
- **Average 3BR Price:** AED 2.5M - AED 3.2M
- **Rental Yield:** 6.8% - 7.5%
- **Occupancy Rate:** 88%
- **Transaction Volume:** Up 25% vs Q4 2023

## Price Trends

Dubai Hills Estate has experienced consistent price appreciation driven by its family-friendly amenities, including Dubai Hills Mall, schools, and extensive green spaces. The community offers a suburban lifestyle within 15 minutes of Downtown Dubai.

## Investment Highlights

1. **Master-Planned Community:** Parks, schools, malls, and golf course
2. **Emaar Quality:** Premium build quality and community management
3. **Family-Oriented:** Strong demand from families with children
4. **Strategic Location:** Easy access to Al Khail Road and Sheikh Zayed Road

## Risk Factors

- Higher prices compared to other suburban communities
- Off-plan purchases carry completion risk
- Limited nightlife and entertainment options

## Recommendation

Dubai Hills Estate is ideal for investors targeting family-oriented tenants seeking quality living. 2BR and 3BR units command strong rental demand. The community offers good capital appreciation potential with lower volatility compared to freehold areas.
